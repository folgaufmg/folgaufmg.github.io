About

The symposium brings together researchers and students to discuss recent results in supergeometry, algebraic geometry and holomorphic foliations, with an emphasis on the interactions between these theories. It is open to everyone interested, and especially to faculty and graduate and undergraduate students from universities in Minas Gerais.

The week combines invited talks by speakers from Brazil and abroad, poster sessions for early-career researchers, a round table on doing science at the public university, and plenty of time for informal discussion and new collaborations.

Supergeometry

Supermanifolds, graded and higher geometric structures, and their links with mathematical physics.

Algebraic Geometry

Moduli spaces, vector bundles and sheaves, and the geometry of projective varieties.

Foliations

Holomorphic foliations and their singularities, and the algebraic and complex geometry around them.

Round table · open to all

Doing science at the public university: access, retention and funding

An open conversation with students and faculty on the path from public schools to research, the role of mathematical olympiads and undergraduate research programs, the conditions for staying in undergraduate and graduate studies, and the underfunding of research at public universities.
